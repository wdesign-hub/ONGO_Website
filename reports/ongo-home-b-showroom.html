Help

Before you book

More answers in the Help centre.

When am I charged?

When you book, we hold the price on your card. You're only charged once the wash is done and you've confirmed it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation is free until the time shown in your booking, and the hold is released.

Who will wash my car?

A licence-verified washer: we check their trade licence, CR and Qatar ID. You'll see their name and can contact them in the app.

Do I need to be there?

[CONFIRM] Leave a parking note so your washer can find the car, then check it and confirm in the app.

Do SUVs cost more?

A little, because bigger cars take longer. Add your car once and every price matches it.
