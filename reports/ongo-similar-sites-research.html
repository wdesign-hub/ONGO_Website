mobilewash.com

MobileWash

New York City · car wash and detailing

Same job as ONGO: a washer comes to your car. Closest on service, far from ONGO on tone.

What it does

  • Dark, cinematic hero with a huge headline. Feels premium, but it hides the price and the app.
  • Two buttons only: Book your wash and View packages. Clear.
  • Price tiers shown on the page ($89, $159, $299 detail packages), plus memberships and a For business block.
  • Service-area section with a Find my technician prompt.

Take for ONGO

  • Show packages and prices early. ONGO already does this.
  • A membership and fleet path is a later add, not a first-version need.

twaddan.com

Twaddan

UAE · on-demand car wash app

A marketplace: you pick a car wash provider, then order. App-first website.

What it does

  • The hero is one line (“On-Demand Car Wash Application”) and the two store buttons. The goal is clear: download.
  • Phone mockups show the provider list, ratings, fees and a live arrival time. This sells the idea in one look.
  • Arabic switch in the top bar. Gulf sites expect it.
  • A separate Register as Provider button for supply side.

Take for ONGO

  • Phone mockups with live arrival and tracking work. ONGO has the screens for this.
  • Add an Arabic switch even if English ships first.

aldobi.com

Aldobi

Qatar · home services super-app (car wash is one of many)

The only Qatar example found. It lists Car Wash next to laundry, cleaning and AC.

What it does

  • Headline: “Qatar’s Trusted Super-App for Home & Personal Services”. Trust words in the sub-line: verified providers, clear pricing.
  • Store buttons right under the headline. Phone mockups below.
  • Sections for testimonials, FAQ and Become a Partner.
  • Because car wash is one tile in a long list, nobody owns “car wash in Doha” on this page.

Take for ONGO

  • ONGO can own the single niche: car wash only, with prices and a time promise.
  • Copy the trust line: verified washers, clear price.

washmen.com

Washmen

UAE · laundry pickup (not car wash)

Not a car wash. Kept as a Gulf example of how a fast, on-demand service sells convenience.

What it does

  • Hero is a checklist of promises: Pick up in 25 minutes, next-day delivery, 4.8 Google rating.
  • Social proof right in the hero: “100,000+ people trust Washmen”.
  • A real person photo, a bright lime accent, a first-order discount (AED 30 off).
  • One strong button: Place your order.

Take for ONGO

  • Put speed and rating in the hero as numbers, like “Arrives in 15–30 min”.
  • A first-wash offer is a common Gulf hook.

What they agree on

  1. Download is the main goal. Store buttons or one booking button sit in the first screen on every site.
  2. Show the app. Phone mockups with arrival time, tracking and price do the selling.
  3. Trust in the hero. Verified providers, ratings, customer counts.
  4. Speed as a number. “25 minutes”, “live arrival”. ONGO has “15–30 min”.
  5. Supply side gets its own button. Register as Provider, Become a Partner.

Where ONGO can win

  1. Car wash only, in Doha. Aldobi is a super-app, the others are not in Qatar.
  2. Real prices up front: Express 45 QAR, Shine 70 QAR, Detailed 120 QAR.
  3. “Card held, charged after the wash” and licence-verified washers. No site above says both.
