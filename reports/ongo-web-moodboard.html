Text on lime is always dark green or ink. White on lime fails contrast (about 1.5:1); white on dark green passes easily.

Type

Clean car. One tap.Space Grotesk for headlines, Inter for body. Geometric and friendly, close to the rounded ONGO wordmark. Headlines heavy and tight, body at 16–18px.

Do

  • Use lime as a highlight: tags, one band, the "Recommended" card.
  • Put real ONGO screens in a hand or on soft lime.
  • Keep one dark green primary button per section.
  • Place proof (rating, "charged after the wash") right under buttons.

Don't

  • Fill whole pages with lime; it tires the eye and weakens buttons.
  • Use stock car-wash photos with foam and sponges; show the app and the result.
  • Invent stats or reviews before you have them.
  • Hide prices behind sign-up.
