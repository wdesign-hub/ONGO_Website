Booking

How do I book a wash?

Open the ONGO app, pin your car’s location, choose a wash and confirm. A licence-verified washer is assigned and you can follow them in the app.

How quickly will a washer arrive?

Usually 15–30 min for a wash booked now. You can also book for a later time.

Do I need to be there?

Leave a parking note so your washer can find the car, then check it and confirm in the app. [CONFIRM whether you must be present]

Can I book for a later time?

Yes. Choose “Later” when you book and pick a time that suits you.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Cancellation is free until the time shown in your booking, and the card hold is released. [CONFIRM how to change a booking]

Payment

When am I charged?

When you book, we hold the price on your card. You’re only charged once the wash is done and you’ve confirmed it.

Which payment methods do you accept?

[CONFIRM accepted cards and wallets] Payment details are handled in the app.

Why is there a hold on my card?

The hold reserves the price of your wash. If you cancel in time, or the wash doesn’t happen, the hold is released. Your bank decides how long a release takes to show.

I was charged the wrong amount.

Contact us with your booking details and we’ll look into it straight away.

Washers

Who will wash my car?

A licence-verified washer. We check their trade licence, CR and Qatar ID before they can take bookings. You’ll see their name and can contact them in the app.

Does my washer see my phone number?

No. Calls and messages go through the app, so your number stays hidden.

What if I’m not happy with the wash?

Tell the washer through the app first, and rate the wash. If it’s still not right, contact us. [CONFIRM re-wash and refund policy]
