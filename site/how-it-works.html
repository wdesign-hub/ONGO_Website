How it works

Three taps to spotless.

Everything happens in the app. You choose the place, the wash and the time. A licence-verified washer comes to your car, and you pay only once it’s done.

Step 1

Pin your car

Tell us where the car is parked. Home, office, mall or the street.

  • LocationDrop a pin or search for the address.
  • Parking noteAdd a note like “Basement B2, spot 14” so your washer finds it first time.
  • Your carAdd your car once. Every price then matches its size.
Step 2

Choose a wash

Pick Express, Shine, Detailed, or build a Custom wash with add-ons.

  • Now or laterBook a washer now, or choose a time that suits you.
  • Clear priceYou see the exact price for your car before you book.
  • Card heldWe hold the price on your card. Nothing is taken yet.
Step 3

Follow, then pay

Watch your washer arrive and work. Check the car, confirm, and only then are you charged.

  • Live statusAssigned, on the way, washing, done.
  • Private contactCall or message through the app. Your number stays hidden.
  • Rate itRate every wash. Your feedback goes straight to the washer.

What happens on the day

Every booking follows the same path, so you always know what comes next.

  • BookedYour card is held for the price shown. You can cancel for free until the time shown in your booking.
  • AssignedA licence-verified washer takes your booking. You see their name.
  • On the wayTypically 15–30 min away. Follow their arrival in the app.
  • WashingThey wash your car where it’s parked. You don’t need to be there [CONFIRM whether customers must be present].
  • DoneCheck the car and confirm in the app. Only then is the held amount charged.

Our promises

The three things we hold ourselves to on every wash.

1

Licence-verified washers

Trade licence, CR and Qatar ID checked before a washer takes a booking.

2

Charged after the wash

The price is held on your card and taken only when you confirm.

3

Free cancellation

Cancel before the time shown in your booking and the hold is released.
