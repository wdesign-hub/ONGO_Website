About ONGO

Car wash that comes to you.

ONGO connects drivers in Doha with licence-verified washers who clean your car where it’s parked, so a clean car never costs you an hour.

Why we built ONGO

Getting a car washed in Doha usually means a detour, a queue and a wait. We thought it should be simpler: you say where the car is, a washer comes to it, and you only pay when you’re happy.

ONGO is the app that makes that happen, for drivers at home, at work and at the mall. [CONFIRM founding story, founders and year]

What we stand for

Three principles shape every part of the app and every washer we work with.

1

Trust

Every washer is licence-verified. We check their trade licence, CR and Qatar ID before they take a booking.

2

Fairness

You see the exact price for your car before you book, and you’re charged only once the wash is done.

3

Clarity

Follow every step live, contact your washer privately and rate each wash. No surprises.

For washers

ONGO gives independent washers a steady flow of bookings, clear prices and fair payment. Washers are verified before they start so drivers can trust who arrives.
